#+title: Manuals - jointhefreeworld.org

This is your guide to using and understanding the free software of the jointhefreeworld project.

Free software is all about giving you control—over the tools you use, how they work, and how you share them. Whether you're just starting out or looking to solve a tricky problem, these manuals are here to help.

Each manual is easy to follow, with clear steps and tips to get you going. You’ll find instructions for setting up, using, and even tweaking the software to fit your needs.

Don’t worry if you’re not a tech expert—these guides are written for everyone. Just pick the manual you need, and let’s get started!
